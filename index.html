Indian Art Through Time

This gallery walks through roughly 4,700 years of art from the Indian subcontinent — from a small bronze dancer buried under the ruins of Mohenjo-daro to an oil-painted heroine from a 19th-century Sanskrit drama. Each stop marks a moment when a new material, technique, or belief system reshaped what Indian artists made and why. Click any node on the timeline below to step inside its gallery label.
